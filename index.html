✦ For You ✦

If this really is the last time we choose to let each other go, I just want you to know that I never stopped caring about you.

I still want you, and a part of me will probably always wish things could have been different between us.

I'll always be grateful for the memories, the time, and the version of us that once made me so happy.

And if we ever meet again someday, I hope we meet as better versions of ourselves. I hope by then, you're serious about what you want, and you know how to love someone without making them question where they stand.

Take care of yourself, Brylle. I loved you, I still do, and maybe that's why letting go hurts this much. But this time, I'm choosing peace too.

This isn't goodbye because I stopped loving you.
It's goodbye because I finally understand that love shouldn't always hurt.
