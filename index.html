For You, One Last Time

If this really is the last time we choose to let each other go, I just want you to know that I never stopped caring about you. I still want you, and a part of me will probably always wish things could have been different between us. But I’m tired of loving you while constantly wondering if I’m enough, if I’m still your choice, or if I’m asking for too much just by wanting reassurance and honesty. I don’t want our love to keep becoming something that hurts us more than it comforts us.

I’m not sending this to make you come back or to change your mind. I just want to leave these words here, because despite everything, what we had was real to me. I’ll always be grateful for the memories, the time, and the version of us that once made me so happy.

And if we ever meet again someday, I hope we meet as better versions of ourselves. I hope by then, you’re serious about what you want, and you know how to love someone without making them question where they stand. If it’s really meant to be us, maybe someday we’ll find our way back to each other—not the same way we did before, but better. And if we don’t, I hope you know that I genuinely wish you a good life. Take care of yourself, Brylle. I loved you, I still do, and maybe that’s why letting go hurts this much. But this time, I’m choosing peace too.

This isn’t goodbye because I stopped loving you. It’s goodbye because I finally understand that love shouldn’t always hurt.
